# Goal Discovery

Use this subskill when a learning request is broad, underspecified, or likely to have several valid interpretations.

Clarify the smallest set of questions that changes the lesson:

- What should the learner be able to do or explain afterward?
- Where will they use it?
- What do they already know or suspect?
- How much time and depth do they want?
- Do they prefer a guided discovery, a direct explanation, examples, practice, or a comparison?

These are open questions, not quizzes. There is no correct answer. Ask one or two at a time, then restate the resulting target in concrete terms before planning.

For a narrow request, infer a reasonable goal and state the assumption instead of interrogating the learner. Stop discovery once another answer would not change the teaching path.
